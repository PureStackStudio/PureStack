<SectionHeader eyebrow="The story behind the site" title="Good things start with a reason." titleTag="h1" titleClass="starter-page-title" subtitle="Use this page to introduce the people and purpose behind your work. A few honest paragraphs are a better beginning than a collection of big claims."/>

<Grid columns="1" columnsLg="minmax(0, 1.2fr) minmax(0, 0.8fr)" alignItems="start" class="gap-6">
  <div>
    <h2 class="mt-0">What brings you here?</h2>
    <p>Start with what you do and the people it is for. Whether this is a personal project, a small business, or a community, give readers enough context to understand what they have found.</p>
    <h2>What do you believe in?</h2>
    <p>Share the point of view behind your work. Describe an approach, a useful principle, or a problem you care about solving. Use specific details that are true for you, and remove any section that does not serve your story.</p>
    <h2>Where should people go next?</h2>
    <p>Give visitors one useful next step: explore your work, read a helpful page, or contact you. Replace the link below with a real destination when it is ready.</p>
    <BtnLink href="/" tone="accent" icon="lucide:arrow-right" iconPosition="end">Explore the site</BtnLink>
  </div>
  <Panel variant="surfaceAlt" class="p-0" bodyClass="p-5">
    <p class="text-eyebrow">A useful writing prompt</p>
    <h2 class="fs-h4 mt-0">Write for one real person.</h2>
    <p class="text-subtle">What would you tell someone discovering your work for the first time? Start there. Keep your own voice.</p>
    <p class="fs-sm m-0">For an AI assistant: use the owner's supplied facts. Ask for missing details instead of inventing customers, results, or a company history.</p>
  </Panel>
</Grid>
