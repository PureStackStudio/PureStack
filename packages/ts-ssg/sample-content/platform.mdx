## Follow a project from start to finish

<Tabs ariaLabel="Waypoint workflow" tone="neutral" variant="flat" tabVariant="underline" class="mb-6">
  <TabPane id="plan" label="Plan" icon="lucide:target">
    <Grid columns="1" columnsMd="2" alignItems="center" class="gap-5">
      <div>
        <h3 class="mt-0">Agree on the destination.</h3>
        <p class="text-subtle mb-0">Write the outcome in plain language. Add a small set of milestones and one accountable owner, so progress has a clear meaning.</p>
      </div>
      <AlertBox title="Example milestone" tone="info" variant="rail">Customer onboarding is ready when the welcome flow, help content, and handoff owner are confirmed.</AlertBox>
    </Grid>
  </TabPane>
  <TabPane id="update" label="Update" icon="lucide:message-circle">
    <Grid columns="1" columnsMd="2" alignItems="center" class="gap-5">
      <div>
        <h3 class="mt-0">Keep the picture current.</h3>
        <p class="text-subtle mb-0">Share what changed, what is blocked, and what happens next. Short updates give the whole team useful context without another status meeting.</p>
      </div>
      <AlertBox title="Example update" tone="success" variant="rail">The help center draft is complete. The only open item is final review of the welcome email.</AlertBox>
    </Grid>
  </TabPane>
  <TabPane id="decide" label="Decide" icon="lucide:git-branch">
    <Grid columns="1" columnsMd="2" alignItems="center" class="gap-5">
      <div>
        <h3 class="mt-0">Make decisions visible.</h3>
        <p class="text-subtle mb-0">Record the options, the person deciding, and the reason for the choice. The next teammate can pick up the work with confidence.</p>
      </div>
      <AlertBox title="Example decision" tone="warning" variant="rail">The launch owner will choose the release date after accessibility review closes.</AlertBox>
    </Grid>
  </TabPane>
</Tabs>
