## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies/local storage and similar technologies.

Under German law, storing or accessing information on user devices generally requires consent unless strictly necessary (TDDDG § 25(1), § 25(2)).

Our categories:

- **Necessary**: required for core functionality and security.
- **Preferences**: optional settings and convenience.
- **Analytics**: measurement and service improvement.
- **Marketing**: advertising and attribution (if used).

You can manage your choices via **Privacy settings** at any time.  
Withdrawing consent is possible at any time and does not affect processing before withdrawal.

## 5. Legal bases summary

Depending on the activity, we rely on:

- GDPR Art. 6(1)(a): consent
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(b): contract/pre-contract
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(c): legal obligation
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(f): legitimate interests

Where processing relies on legitimate interests, our interests include secure and reliable operation, fraud prevention, and efficient service delivery.

## 10. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (GDPR Art. 77), in particular in your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

For Germany, competent authorities may include:

- your relevant State ("Land") data protection authority for most private-sector entities
- BfDI for specific federal/telecom/postal contexts

See:

- **BfDI complaint information**: <https://www.bfdi.bund.de/EN/Buerger/Inhalte/Allgemein/Datenschutz/BeschwerdeBeiDatenschutzbehoerden.html>
