# LandingBand Samples

`LandingBand` creates a full-bleed page band for hero breaks, campaign moments,
image strips, and section transitions. It can stay rectangular, or shape either
edge with a slant.

## 7. Usage pattern

```html
<LandingBand
  tone="accent"
  variant="surfaceAlt"
  height="auto"
  paddingTop="4rem"
  paddingBottom="4rem"
  topEdge="flat|slant-up|slant-down"
  topEdgeStart="0%|50%|200px|calc(50% + 2rem)"
  bottomEdge="flat|slant-up|slant-down"
  bottomEdgeStart="0%|50%|200px|calc(50% + 2rem)"
  edgeSize="4rem"
  image="/hero.png"
  imageFit="cover"
  imagePosition="center"
>
  <SectionHeader
    title="Shape the page section."
    subtitle="Put regular content inside the band."/>
</LandingBand>
```
