# Launch checklist

The starter is intentionally complete enough to explore, but Waypoint is a fictional product. Use this checklist to turn the example into a site that describes a real offering accurately.

## Brand and content

- Replace the Waypoint name, logo, site title, and description in `siteConfig.json`.
- Rewrite the home promise, platform claims, calls to action, and footer for your audience.
- Replace all illustrative workspace data, plan details, and journal content.
- Remove any page that does not help your visitors make a decision or complete a task.

## Working journeys

- Connect the contact form to an endpoint and verify success and error states.
- Replace example plan links with your real sales or checkout flow.
- If you add sign-in, connect it to a real identity system and protect private routes on the server.
- Check every internal link and every external destination.

## Publishing settings

- Set your real public URL before enabling sitemap and robots generation.
- Add accurate social preview metadata and a preview image for your brand.
- Publish the legal and privacy pages that apply to your organization and data use.
- Enable analytics or other optional services only after their consent and policy behavior is configured.
- Review search results and exclude pages that should not be indexed.

## Final review

Read the site from the home page as a first-time visitor. Verify that each page has one clear purpose, headings follow a useful order, charts have meaningful descriptions, forms can be completed by keyboard, and mobile layouts remain readable.

<AlertBox title="The best launch check" tone="success">
  Ask someone outside the project to explain what the site offers and what they would do next. Revise any page that makes them guess.
</AlertBox>
