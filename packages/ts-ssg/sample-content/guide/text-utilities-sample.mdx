# Text Utility Samples

These text utilities are meant to unify voice across the site while staying
theme-aware. They read from the current semantic tone, so they compose nicely
with `Panel` and other tone-aware surfaces.

## 6. Raw editorial flow

<span class="text-eyebrow">Editorial rhythm</span>
<h2 class="text-title">This section uses only normal document flow</h2>
<p class="text-tagline">
  No `Flex`, no `Grid`, and no spacing helpers. This is the baseline spacing
  behavior of the text utilities when they are dropped into content directly.
</p>
<p>
  That makes this section the best place to judge whether the defaults feel
  balanced. If something feels too tight or too loose here, we should fix the
  utility itself instead of compensating in every page.
</p>
<p>
  The goal is to make rich content feel polished by default, so authors can
  focus on hierarchy and meaning instead of local spacing corrections.
</p>
