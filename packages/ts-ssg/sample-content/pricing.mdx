## Questions a pricing page should answer

<ExpandablePanel title="Can a team change plans later?" description="Explain the upgrade path in direct language." icon="lucide:arrow-right" tone="neutral" open="true">
  <p>In a real product, state when a plan change takes effect, how billing is adjusted, and what happens to existing data. This starter leaves those business rules to you.</p>
</ExpandablePanel>

<ExpandablePanel title="What counts as a person?" description="Define the unit behind per-person pricing." icon="lucide:users-round" tone="neutral">
  <p>Specify whether guests, viewers, and suspended members are billed. Link to the same definition from checkout and account settings.</p>
</ExpandablePanel>

<ExpandablePanel title="What is included in support?" description="Set expectations before a customer needs help." icon="lucide:circle-question-mark" tone="neutral">
  <p>Describe channels, hours, response targets, and any onboarding services in your actual offer.</p>
</ExpandablePanel>

<AlertBox title="Before launch" tone="info">
  Replace every illustrative amount and entitlement. Connect the calls to action to a working sales or checkout flow and publish your real terms.
</AlertBox>
