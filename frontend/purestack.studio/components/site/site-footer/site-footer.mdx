## Behavior and accessibility

The default teleport destination is body, so ordinary usage moves the footer out of the local content tree. Supply a target selector for inline placement. Its bottom row adapts from stacked content to a horizontal layout.

<ApiProperty anchor="site-footer-prop-teleport" name="teleport" valueType="string" defaultValue="body">
  <p>Selector of the footer’s destination. A falsy value falls back to body; use an explicit local target for a contained example.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="site-footer-prop-ariaLabel" name="ariaLabel" valueType="string" defaultValue="Site footer">
  <p>Accessible name on the footer.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="site-footer-prop-copyright" name="copyright" valueType="string" defaultValue="current year and All rights reserved">
  <p>Copyright text; an empty value uses the built-in fallback.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="site-footer-prop-legalLabel" name="legalLabel" valueType="string" defaultValue="Legal and policy links">
  <p>Accessible name on the legal navigation.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="site-footer-prop-tone" name="tone" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;SemanticTone&gt;" defaultValue="inherited">
  <p>Semantic intent: neutral, accent, secondary, info, success, warning, danger, feature, custom or ghost. The active skin supplies the colors.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="site-footer-prop-variant" name="variant" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;ComponentVariant&gt;" defaultValue="surface">
  <p>Visual treatment. Accepts solid, surface, surfaceAlt, spotlight, glass, flat, flatAlt, flatSolid, outlineFill, outline, subtle, subtleBtn, link, sheen, underline, rail, bracket or none.</p>
</ApiProperty>

### Composition

<div class="api-contract">
<p><strong>Default slot:</strong> main footer content. <strong>legal:</strong> legal or policy links. <strong>social:</strong> additional links. The legal row includes the consent-settings teleport target. Register defineFooterComponents and defineFlexComponents.</p>
</div>
