## Behavior and accessibility

Titles render as h3. A comparison column can contain links but is not itself clickable. Keep feature order consistent across sibling columns.

<ApiProperty anchor="comparison-column-prop-title" name="title" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>Visible heading. Choose a short title that identifies the content or task.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="comparison-column-prop-summary" name="summary" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>A concise explanation beneath the heading.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="comparison-column-prop-badge" name="badge" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>Optional compact label rendered with Badge.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="comparison-column-prop-icon" name="icon" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>Registered SVG icon name. In browser apps, include the icon in the resolver passed to defineIconComponents.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="comparison-column-prop-tone" name="tone" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;SemanticTone&gt;" defaultValue="inherited">
  <p>Semantic intent: neutral, accent, secondary, info, success, warning, danger, feature, custom or ghost. The active skin supplies the colors.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="comparison-column-prop-variant" name="variant" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;ComponentVariant&gt;" defaultValue="surface">
  <p>Visual treatment. Accepts solid, surface, surfaceAlt, spotlight, glass, flat, flatAlt, flatSolid, outlineFill, outline, subtle, subtleBtn, link, sheen, underline, rail, bracket or none.</p>
</ApiProperty>
