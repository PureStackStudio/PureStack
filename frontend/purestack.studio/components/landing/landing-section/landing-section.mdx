## A section that tells a story

LandingSection brings a heading, supporting copy, and content together with
consistent spacing. Use it for a product introduction, a feature collection, or
the next chapter in your landing page. The heading sits above the content by
default, so adding a card never changes where the heading belongs.

The component uses built-in spacing and typography utilities. Add your own
styles through the `landing-section` class when your site needs a different
treatment. The examples below include styling from this site's design.

This centered introduction leads into three features. `columnsLg="3"` gives the
content three columns on large screens; smaller screens keep a single column.

## Feature map

| Public feature | Visible example |
| --- | --- |
| tone, variant, variantMode | Complete galleries above, with all supported values visible together. |
| eyebrow, title, subtitle | Introduce the section with a short label, a heading, and supporting copy, or omit them for content alone. |
| titleTag | All nine supported elements are rendered in the heading-semantics comparison. |
| layout, align | Stack or split the introduction and content; align the introduction to the start or center. |
| columns, columnsLg | Set the content columns without changing the heading layout. |
| alignItems | Start, center, end, and stretch are shown together. |
| Default slot, actions slot | Place your content in the default slot and heading links in actions. |
| Native attributes and events | Pass attributes through the root; keyboard behavior belongs to the composed native controls. |

## Behavior and accessibility

Use titleTag to fit the heading into the document. Content stacks on smaller
screens. Both columns and columnsLg configure the content grid on large
screens, with columnsLg taking precedence. The heading stays outside that grid.

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-subtitle"
  name="subtitle"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;"
  defaultValue="not set"
>
  <p>Supporting text beneath the title.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-titleTag"
  name="titleTag"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;LandingTitleTag&gt;"
  defaultValue="h2"
>
  <p>
    Heading element. Accepts h1–h6, p, div or span; invalid values fall back to
    h2. Match the surrounding document hierarchy.
  </p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-tone"
  name="tone"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;SemanticTone&gt;"
  defaultValue="inherited"
>
  <p>
    Semantic intent: neutral, accent, secondary, info, success, warning, danger,
    feature, custom or ghost. The active skin supplies the colors.
  </p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-variant"
  name="variant"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;ComponentVariant&gt;"
  defaultValue="none"
>
  <p>
    Visual treatment. Accepts solid, surface, surfaceAlt, spotlight, glass,
    flat, flatAlt, flatSolid, outlineFill, outline, subtle, subtleBtn, link,
    sheen, underline, rail, bracket or none.
  </p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-variantMode"
  name="variantMode"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;ComponentVariantMode&gt;"
  defaultValue="stateless"
>
  <p>
    Use stateless for content surfaces; stateful enables the treatment’s hover,
    focus and active styles. It does not add interaction handlers.
  </p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-layout"
  name="layout"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;'stacked' | 'split'&gt;"
  defaultValue="stacked"
>
  <p>
    Keep the introduction above the content, or place them side by side when
    space allows.
  </p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-align"
  name="align"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;'start' | 'center'&gt;"
  defaultValue="start"
>
  <p>
    Align the heading, supporting copy, and actions. Content keeps its own
    alignment.
  </p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-columns"
  name="columns"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;number | string&gt;"
  defaultValue="not set"
>
  <p>
    Content columns on large screens, as a count or CSS track definition.
    Smaller screens use one column.
  </p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-columnsLg"
  name="columnsLg"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;number | string&gt;"
  defaultValue="columns, then 1"
>
  <p>
    Content columns on large screens. Takes precedence over columns. The heading
    has its own space and does not count as a content column.
  </p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty
  anchor="landing-section-prop-alignItems"
  name="alignItems"
  valueType="RefOrValue&lt;GridAlignItems&gt;"
  defaultValue="start"
>
  <p>
    Vertical alignment of the introduction and content, and items within the
    content grid: stretch, start, center or end.
  </p>
</ApiProperty>
