## Behavior and accessibility

Use clear billing text and disclose relevant limits alongside the price. A CTA is rendered only when both ctaLabel and ctaLink are set. There is no checkout behavior built into the component.

</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-summary" name="summary" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>A concise explanation beneath the heading.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-price" name="price" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>Display price. May include currency or non-numeric text.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-period" name="period" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>Billing period displayed with the price.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-badge" name="badge" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>Optional compact label rendered with Badge.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-note" name="note" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>Supporting note after the features and action.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-variant" name="variant" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;ComponentVariant&gt;" defaultValue="surface">
  <p>Visual treatment. Accepts solid, surface, surfaceAlt, spotlight, glass, flat, flatAlt, flatSolid, outlineFill, outline, subtle, subtleBtn, link, sheen, underline, rail, bracket or none.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-variantMode" name="variantMode" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;ComponentVariantMode&gt;" defaultValue="stateless">
  <p>Use stateless for content surfaces; stateful enables the treatment’s hover, focus and active styles. It does not add interaction handlers.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-icon" name="icon" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>Registered SVG icon name. In browser apps, include the icon in the resolver passed to defineIconComponents.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-ctaLabel" name="ctaLabel" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>CTA link text. Requires ctaLink.</p>
</ApiProperty>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-ctaLink" name="ctaLink" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;string&gt;" defaultValue="not set">
  <p>CTA destination. Requires ctaLabel.</p>

<ApiProperty anchor="pricing-plan-prop-tone" name="tone" valueType="RefOrValue&lt;SemanticTone&gt;" defaultValue="inherited">
  <p>Semantic intent: neutral, accent, secondary, info, success, warning, danger, feature, custom or ghost. The active skin supplies the colors.</p>
