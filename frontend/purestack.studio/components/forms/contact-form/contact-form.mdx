## Every configurable text layer

ContactForm has no tone or variant prop. Its configurable layers are title, description, submit label, four field labels, three text placeholders, and three topic labels. The preview below shows all of them together; the working form above captures a local submission.

<ComponentExample label="A complete copy configuration" caption="This disabled copy sample demonstrates the text API without submitting to an endpoint.">
<fieldset disabled class="forms-copy-sample"><legend>Configured copy</legend><ContactForm title="Start a conversation" description="Tell us what you want to build." action="#" method="post" submitLabel="Send project brief" nameLabel="Your name" emailLabel="Contact email" topicLabel="What brings you here?" messageLabel="Project context" namePlaceholder="Alex Morgan" emailPlaceholder="alex@example.com" messagePlaceholder="Describe your goals and constraints." topicGeneralLabel="New project" topicSupportLabel="Product support" topicPartnershipLabel="Work together"/></fieldset>
</ComponentExample>

```html
<ContactForm action="/api/contact" method="post" title="Start a conversation"
  description="Tell us what you want to build." submitLabel="Send project brief"
  nameLabel="Your name" emailLabel="Contact email" topicLabel="Topic" messageLabel="Project context"
  namePlaceholder="Alex Morgan" emailPlaceholder="alex@example.com" messagePlaceholder="Your goals"
  topicGeneralLabel="New project" topicSupportLabel="Support" topicPartnershipLabel="Work together"/>
```

The method accepts get or post and defaults to post. The native form uses text/plain encoding; choose an endpoint that accepts that format or intercept submission to build a different request.

## Behavior and accessibility

The component renders a native form with required fields and enctype text/plain. Its props are resolved when the component is created, rather than designed as reactive refs. An action URL alone is not a delivery service; configure an endpoint that accepts the form’s encoding.
